# 🚀 VentureLink | Universal Micro-Venture Marketplace

> **Democratizing early-stage funding for neighborhood shops, physical makers, and digital startups.**

VentureLink bridges the gap between ambitious builders and micro-investors. Unlike traditional venture platforms tailored strictly for high-growth tech equity, VentureLink enables flexible deal structures—from monthly revenue-share royalties for physical businesses to milestone-driven equity for digital projects.

### 🌟 Key Highlights
- **Adaptive Deal Structuring:** Auto-calculates revenue-share payback models for brick-and-mortar retail and standard equity terms for tech ventures.
- **AI-Powered Pitch Polisher:** Leverages Gemini to transform raw founder ideas into structured, professional investor executive summaries.
- **Milestone-Based Escrow:** Protects investor capital by releasing funds against verifiable execution proofs and real-world milestones.
- **Smart Thesis Matching:** Pairs founders and backers using categorized criteria (category, geography, check size, and preferred return model).
